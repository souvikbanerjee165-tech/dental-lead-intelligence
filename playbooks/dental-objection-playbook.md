# Dental Practice Objection Handling Playbook (LAER Framework)

> Adapted from enterprise sales frameworks (ai-sales-team-claude) tailored specifically for Dental Clinics, DSOs, and Owner-Operated Dental Practices.
> **LAER Framework**: **L**isten • **A**cknowledge • **E**xplore • **R**espond

---

## Front Desk & Gatekeeper Objections

### 1. "The Doctor is with a patient / in operatory right now."
**Category:** Gatekeeper Access  
**Frequency:** Very High (80%+ of cold dials)  
- **Acknowledge:** "Totally understand — clinical care comes first, I wouldn't expect Dr. [DOCTOR_NAME] to step away from a patient."
- **Explore:** "Just to be respectful of their schedule, who typically reviews patient communication technology when patients call after hours — is that you, or does Dr. [DOCTOR_NAME] handle that directly?"
- **Respond:** "The reason I'm reaching out is that our audit of your local area showed that when patients call between 5 PM and 8 AM, or on weekends, there's no live conversational intake. We help nearby practices capture roughly 4 to 8 high-value emergency or implant patients a month who would otherwise call the next clinic on Google."
- **Redirect:** "I can send a 90-second video teardown showing exactly where those calls are dropping off. What's the best direct email address to send that over to for you and Dr. [DOCTOR_NAME]?"

---

### 2. "We already use Weave / Podium / RevenueWell."
**Category:** Incumbent Tech Stack  
**Frequency:** Very High  
- **Acknowledge:** "Weave and Podium are fantastic platforms — we actually love practices that use them because it means your team already values modern patient engagement."
- **Explore:** "When a patient calls your office at 8:30 on a Saturday night with acute tooth pain or looking for an all-on-4 consultation, does Weave pick up the phone, converse with them naturally, and book them into your Eaglesoft/Dentrix calendar, or does it just send an automated one-way SMS saying 'we are closed'?"
- **Respond:** "We don't replace Weave's VoIP phones or two-way texting. We sit directly on top of it as the intelligent voice receptionist for after-hours and overflow when both your front desk lines are busy. Instead of a patient hanging up and calling a competitor, our voice agent answers on ring 2, triage their situation, and secures the booking."
- **Redirect:** "Could I show you a 3-minute comparison of how we integrate alongside your existing phone system without changing your phone numbers?"

---

### 3. "Our front desk team handles all our incoming calls just fine."
**Category:** Perceived Lack of Pain  
**Frequency:** High  
- **Acknowledge:** "That's fantastic. Having a dedicated front office coordinator makes all the difference for in-clinic patient experience."
- **Explore:** "What happens when your receptionist is checking in a patient at the front desk, processing an insurance claim, and two new patient phone calls ring simultaneously? Which one gets prioritized?"
- **Respond:** "Industry data across dental practices shows that 34% of incoming calls ring while staff are already on the phone or assisting a patient face-to-face. Those calls roll to voicemail, and 85% of first-time callers seeking implants or cosmetic work never leave a voicemail — they click the next practice."
- **Redirect:** "What if we just gave your front desk backup coverage for overflow and lunch hours so your team never feels overwhelmed?"

---

### 4. "Just send an email to info@clinic.com."
**Category:** Brush-Off  
**Frequency:** High  
- **Acknowledge:** "I'm happy to do that so you have complete documentation."
- **Explore:** "I want to make sure I don't send generic spam. What's the single biggest friction point right now — after-hours patient intake, or front-desk phone overload during peak morning hours?"
- **Respond:** "I've generated a customized 1-page financial leakage report specifically for [CLINIC_NAME] calculating the estimated monthly chair revenue lost to unanswered after-hours calls. I want to make sure it gets to Dr. [DOCTOR_NAME]'s desk and doesn't get buried in the general inbox."
- **Redirect:** "If I send it to your direct email right now, could we reconnect for 60 seconds tomorrow afternoon once you've had a quick glance?"

## Doctor & Practice Principal Objections

### 5. "Patients want to talk to real human beings, not robots."
**Category:** Patient Trust & Clinical Reputation  
**Frequency:** Common  
- **Acknowledge:** "100% agreed. Dentistry is personal, and patient trust is everything to Dr. [DOCTOR_NAME]."
- **Explore:** "When a patient calls with a broken tooth at 9 PM on a Sunday, what is their current human experience? Is it a human, or is it an answering machine greeting saying 'Leave a message and we'll call you back Monday morning'?"
- **Respond:** "Our AI sounds completely natural with human cadence, warmth, and clinical empathy. It's not a rigid robotic tree ('Press 1 for hours'). It converses like an empathetic receptionist, calms the patient down, answers FAQs, and books them. That beats an impersonal voicemail machine 100% of the time."
- **Redirect:** "Would you be open to dialing our live interactive demonstration line right now for 30 seconds to hear the voice fidelity firsthand?"

---

### 6. "How much does it cost? We don't have budget for expensive software."
**Category:** Price / ROI  
**Frequency:** Common  
- **Acknowledge:** "Completely fair — every technology investment in a practice needs to show clear ROI."
- **Explore:** "What is an average new patient worth to your practice in the first year? Usually in general dentistry it's around $850 to $1,200, and for clear aligners or implants it's $3,500 to $5,000+."
- **Respond:** "Our service starts at a flat monthly rate of $497 to $997. That means if the AI captures just ONE new patient every 30 to 60 days that would have otherwise hung up and called another dentist, your investment is already cash-flow positive. Any additional patients captured go straight to practice margin."
- **Redirect:** "Let's run a quick 3-variable calculation based on your actual patient case values. If the numbers don't show at least 5x return, you shouldn't buy it."

---

### 7. "Will this integrate with our practice management system (Dentrix / Eaglesoft / Open Dental)?"
**Category:** Technical Compatibility  
**Frequency:** High  
- **Acknowledge:** "Crucial question — nobody wants double-entry or duplicate charting."
- **Explore:** "Which version of PMS are you running, and do you currently use an online scheduler like LocalMed or NexHealth?"
- **Respond:** "We integrate seamlessly with Dentrix, Eaglesoft, Open Dental, NexHealth, and LocalMed. The AI checks real-time operatory and provider availability, slots the appointment directly into the designated appointment book, and writes a detailed note summarizing the patient's symptoms and insurance provider."
- **Redirect:** "We can configure the AI to simply notify your front desk with full patient details for approval, or push directly to your schedule based on your clinical preference."
