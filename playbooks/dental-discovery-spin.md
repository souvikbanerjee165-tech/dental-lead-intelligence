# Dental Consultative SPIN Selling Discovery Playbook

> Adapted from ai-dialer & enterprise consultative sales systems for Dental Practice Outreach.
> **SPIN Model**: **S**ituation • **P**roblem • **I**mplication • **N**eed-Payoff

---

## Stage 1: Situation Questions (Establish Clinical Context)
*Goal: Understand practice flow without interrogating the receptionist or doctor.*

1. "How many operatories and active providers do you currently have running at [CLINIC_NAME]?"
2. "Who typically answers the incoming patient line when the front office is checking patients in or out?"
3. "Do you currently offer online appointment scheduling, or do all appointments require a phone call?"
4. "What practice management software are you using to manage your chair schedule — Dentrix, Eaglesoft, or Open Dental?"

---

## Stage 2: Problem Questions (Uncover Unaddressed Bottlenecks)
*Goal: Bring latent friction to the surface.*

1. "What happens on Friday evenings and weekends when prospective patients call with urgent dental questions or tooth pain?"
2. "How often does your front desk team find itself juggling an in-person patient and a ringing telephone at the same time?"
3. "When prospective patients leave a voicemail after hours, what percentage of them do you estimate actually answer when you call back Monday morning?"
4. "Have you noticed high-intent prospective patients asking for Invisalign or cosmetic consultations giving up because they couldn't get a prompt appointment?"

---

## Stage 3: Implication Questions (Quantify Commercial Pain)
*Goal: Elevate the cost of inaction from an inconvenience to an executive emergency.*

1. "If a prospective patient with a broken crown calls at 7 PM on a Thursday and gets your voicemail, where do you think they go next? Do they wait, or do they immediately call another practice down the street?"
2. "If your practice loses just 2 or 3 of those emergency or elective procedure calls per month, what does that cost the clinic annually in chair revenue? (Usually $25,000 to $45,000+)"
3. "How much stress and burnout is the front desk staff experiencing when Mondays start with a backlog of 30 missed calls and unread messages?"
4. "If you're spending money on Google Ads or local SEO, does it frustrate you that marketing dollars are driving clicks to a phone line that nobody answers after 5 PM?"

---

## Stage 4: Need-Payoff Questions (Lead Doctor/Manager to Solution)
*Goal: Have the client articulate the value of adopting an AI Voice Receptionist.*

1. "If every after-hours caller was greeted immediately by an intelligent voice assistant that answered their insurance questions and booked them directly into your calendar, how would that impact your monthly production?"
2. "How much easier would Monday mornings be for your team if all weekend calls were already sorted, scheduled, and categorized with full notes?"
3. "Would capturing an extra $4,000 to $8,000 in monthly new patient production justify a system that costs a fraction of a single part-time receptionist?"
4. "If we could set this up as a pilot over a weekend without touching your current phone carrier or numbers, would you be open to seeing how many patients it captures?"
