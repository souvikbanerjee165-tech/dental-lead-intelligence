# Dental Executive Demo & Meeting Preparation Brief

> Adapted from ai-sales-team-claude enterprise meeting prep framework.
> Pre-call checklist before meeting with Dental Practice Owner or Practice Administrator.

---

## 1. Practice Reconnaissance Checklist
- [ ] **Doctor / Owner**: Verified name, DDS/DMD credentials, dental school, practice tenure.
- [ ] **Practice Scale**: Number of operatories, associate dentists, hygienists.
- [ ] **PMS / EHR Stack**: Dentrix, Eaglesoft, Open Dental, Curve, Carestream.
- [ ] **Patient Sentiment Mining**: Any Google review complaints regarding long hold times, missed weekend calls, or receptionist demeanor.
- [ ] **High-Ticket Procedures**: Implant dentistry, cosmetic veneers, Invisalign/SureSmile, sleep apnea appliances.
- [ ] **Current After-Hours Experience**: Tested live — does it go to generic voicemail, third-party answering service, or dead tone?

---

## 2. Calculated Practice Economics
- **Estimated Monthly Patient Leakage**: `$3,500 – $7,500/mo` (Based on 1.2 missed calls/day * 20% conversion * $850 avg initial case value).
- **Annual Production Leakage**: `$42,000 – $90,000/yr`.
- **Target Recovery Target**: 4–6 additional high-intent patients per month.

---

## 3. Demo Agenda (15-Minute Target)
1. **Rapport & Verification (2 mins)**:
   - "Thanks for taking the time, Dr. [DOCTOR_NAME]. I pulled your practice analytics in [CITY] — you've got an impressive [RATING]★ reputation across [REVIEW_COUNT] reviews."
2. **Current Bottleneck Validation (3 mins)**:
   - "When we dialed your clinic after 5:30 PM yesterday, we noted calls roll to voicemail. In your area, ~42% of dental queries happen outside 9-to-5. Are you currently tracking how many of those callers actually callback?"
3. **Interactive AI Receptionist Demonstration (5 mins)**:
   - Dial the live demo number. Put speakerphone on. Show how the AI handles a Saturday emergency caller with a chipped molar seeking urgent booking.
   - Show how the AI detects clinical urgency, captures insurance, and offers immediate appointment slots.
4. **Integration & PMS Workflow (3 mins)**:
   - Show Eaglesoft / Dentrix sync workflow: no disruption to front desk, zero duplicate data entry.
5. **Next Step & Pilot Lock-in (2 mins)**:
   - "Let's do a 14-day weekend overflow pilot. If it doesn't capture at least 3 qualified patient bookings, you owe us nothing."
